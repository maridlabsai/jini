# Research To PRD Benchmark Context

This benchmark context models a common product-delivery problem:

- research exists across interviews, support tickets, analytics, and competitor notes
- the team needs a sharp PRD, not another research dump
- engineering wants requirements and tasks that can be trusted

## Distilled scenario

The target product is Jini itself.

Question:

How should Jini turn research into a product surface that buyers and engineers
both trust?

Validated observations:

- teams lose momentum when research, PRD writing, and engineering planning are separate workflows
- unsupported PRD claims create rework and mistrust downstream
- product teams prefer a compact default path over a large menu of optional process steps
- the strongest near-term differentiator is a research-backed bridge into build-ready execution

Expected outcome:

- canonical research artifacts
- a crisp Brief
- a rendered PRD view
- a build-ready Spec, Plan, and Tasks handoff
