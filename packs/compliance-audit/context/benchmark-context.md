# Compliance Audit Benchmark Context

The organization needs a lightweight but explicit audit workflow for regulated
or control-heavy review surfaces. Reviewers need a compact plan that ties
scope, evidence, remediation, residual risk, and approval readiness together.

The benchmark context is intentionally local-first:

- evidence should remain tied to canonical artifacts and later harvest outputs
- approval should remain explicit rather than buried in chat or email
- publication and handoff should stay portable through wiki or issue surfaces

The pack should compile into a usable audit plan with no manual YAML repair.
