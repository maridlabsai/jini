# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Use direct, respectful, technically honest communication.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, and bad
faith behavior are not.

## Expected Behavior

- critique ideas clearly
- cite evidence where possible
- keep discussion focused on the work
- avoid hostility and performative escalation

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or intimidation
- personal insults
- discriminatory language
- spam or low-effort abuse

Maintainers may moderate discussions, close threads, or remove participants who
repeatedly violate these standards.
