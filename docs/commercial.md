<p class="page-lead">The core Jini tool stays public. Paid product adds hosted convenience, continuity, shared controls, and governance. Paid services help teams adopt and operate Jini well.</p>

<div class="checklist-grid">
  <div class="checklist-card">
    <h3>What stays public</h3>
    <p>Runtime, docs, examples, tests, local SLM use, and BYO provider use stay in the public product surface.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="checklist-card">
    <h3>What paid product adds</h3>
    <p>Hosted routing, sync, background execution, premium connectors, shared workflows, and governance controls.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="checklist-card">
    <h3>What paid services add</h3>
    <p>Onboarding, rollout help, custom integrations, implementation support, and enterprise support programs.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="checklist-grid">
  <div class="checklist-card">
    <h3>The rule</h3>
    <p><strong>Do not charge for basic access to the core tool. Charge for hosted convenience, shared control, and support.</strong></p>
  </div>
  <div class="checklist-card">
    <h3>Why upgrade</h3>
    <p>Upgrade when you want Jini-managed continuity and hosted execution without setting up or managing every provider yourself.</p>
  </div>
</div>
