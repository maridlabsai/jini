<p class="page-lead">These examples answer the three questions most people actually care about: what they already have, what they ask Jini for, and what they get back next.</p>

<div class="scenario-grid">
  <div class="scenario-card" id="meeting-followup">
    <span class="section-kicker">After a meeting</span>
    <h3>Turn notes into a sendable follow-up</h3>
    <div class="scenario-subgrid">
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You already have</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>notes</li>
          <li>loose decisions</li>
          <li>implied owners</li>
          <li>action items that are not clean yet</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You ask</strong>
        <p><code>turn these notes into a follow-up I can send</code></p>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>Jini gives back</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>Sendable Follow-up</li>
          <li>Owners and Due Points</li>
          <li>Task List</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You do next</strong>
        <p>Review the sendable note, confirm owners and due dates, and keep any missing pieces visible before sending it.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="scenario-card" id="spec-readiness">

<div class="section-card">
  <span class="section-kicker">Why this should feel better</span>
  <h2>What Jini should do better than plain chat</h2>
  <div class="proof-grid">
    <div class="proof-card">
      <h3>Give you something usable quickly</h3>
      <p>The output should come before the summary.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="proof-card">
      <h3>Keep what is missing visible</h3>
      <p>Missing proof, blockers, and uncertainties should stay nearby instead of getting buried.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="proof-card">
      <h3>Keep the next move obvious</h3>
      <p>The user should know whether to review, clarify, approve, or continue.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <p>If Jini only gives you a cleaner explanation of the work, it is not good enough.</p>
</div>
