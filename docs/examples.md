<p class="page-lead">These examples answer the three questions most people actually care about: what they already have, what they ask Jini for, and what they get back next.</p>

<div class="section-card section-card-story" markdown="1">
  <span class="section-kicker">Five familiar jobs</span>
  <h2>Start from work people already recognize</h2>
  <p class="page-lead">The examples should feel like the homepage promise made specific: familiar input, one clear ask, one usable artifact, and an obvious next move.</p>

<div class="scenario-grid">
  <div class="scenario-card" id="meeting-followup">
    <span class="section-kicker">After a meeting</span>
    <h3>Turn notes into a sendable follow-up</h3>
    <div class="scenario-subgrid">
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You already have</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>notes</li>
          <li>loose decisions</li>
          <li>implied owners</li>
          <li>action items that are not clean yet</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You ask</strong>
        <p><code>turn these notes into a follow-up I can send</code></p>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>Jini gives back</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>Sendable Follow-up</li>
          <li>Owners and Due Points</li>
          <li>Task List</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You do next</strong>
        <p>Review the sendable note, confirm owners and due dates, and keep any missing pieces visible before sending it.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="scenario-card" id="spec-readiness">
    <span class="section-kicker">Before a handoff</span>
    <h3>Check whether a plan is actually ready</h3>
    <div class="scenario-subgrid">
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You already have</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>a draft</li>
          <li>comments</li>
          <li>a handoff that feels almost done</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You ask</strong>
        <p><code>check whether this plan is ready to hand off</code></p>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>Jini gives back</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>Build-Readiness Check</li>
          <li>Handoff Brief</li>
          <li>Missing Pieces Before Build</li>
          <li>Task List</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You do next</strong>
        <p>Open the readiness check first, clear the missing pieces, and avoid shipping a plan that only looks finished.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="scenario-card" id="paris-trip">
    <span class="section-kicker">Before a trip</span>
    <h3>Plan a 7 day Paris trip without losing the thread</h3>
    <div class="scenario-subgrid">
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You already have</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>travel dates</li>
          <li>rough budget</li>
          <li>the kind of trip you want</li>
          <li>anything that must be included</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You ask</strong>
        <p><code>plan a 7 day Paris trip with a clear day-by-day itinerary, likely costs, and anything I still need to decide</code></p>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>Jini gives back</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>7 Day Paris Trip</li>
          <li>Likely Costs</li>
          <li>What You Still Need To Decide</li>
          <li>Booking Checklist</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You do next</strong>
        <p>Open the itinerary first, review the missing choices before booking, and keep the checklist visible instead of rebuilding it later.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="scenario-card" id="vendor-choice">
    <span class="section-kicker">Before a decision</span>
    <h3>Choose one option and keep the reasoning attached</h3>
    <div class="scenario-subgrid">
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You already have</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>several options</li>
          <li>your criteria</li>
          <li>tradeoffs people are debating</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You ask</strong>
        <p><code>compare these options and give me a recommendation I can defend</code></p>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>Jini gives back</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>Recommendation Memo</li>
          <li>Tradeoff Table</li>
          <li>Questions Still Open</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You do next</strong>
        <p>Review the recommendation, see where the decision is still weak, and keep the reasoning attached for later review.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="scenario-card" id="incident-closure">
    <span class="section-kicker">Before real closure</span>
    <h3>Close work cleanly, not just quickly</h3>
    <div class="scenario-subgrid">
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You already have</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">

          <li>important follow-up may still be missing</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You ask</strong>
        <p><code>show me what still has to happen before this is really closed</code></p>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>Jini gives back</strong>
        <ul class="compact-list">
          <li>Closure Checklist</li>

        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="scenario-block">
        <strong>You do next</strong>
